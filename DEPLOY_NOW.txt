═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  STEP 2: DEPLOY ON RAILWAY (THE EASY WAY!)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to: https://railway.app

2. Click "Login" (top-right)
   → Sign in with GitHub
   → Authorize Railway

3. Click "New Project"
   → "Deploy from GitHub repo"
   → Select: NannAmmZem/minimart-ecommerce
   → Click "Deploy Now"

4. Add Database:
   → Click "New" (in your project)
   → Select "Database"
   → Choose "Add MySQL"
   → Wait for database to provision (30 seconds)

5. Configure Laravel Service:
   → Click on your "minimart-ecommerce" service
   → Go to "Variables" tab
   → Click "Raw Editor"
   → Paste this (Railway will auto-connect database):

APP_NAME=MiniMart
APP_ENV=production
APP_KEY=base64:YOUR_KEY_FROM_LOCAL_ENV
APP_DEBUG=false
APP_URL=${{RAILWAY_PUBLIC_DOMAIN}}
DB_CONNECTION=mysql
DB_HOST=${{MYSQL.RAILWAY_PRIVATE_DOMAIN}}
DB_PORT=3306
DB_DATABASE=${{MYSQL.MYSQL_DATABASE}}
DB_USERNAME=${{MYSQL.MYSQL_USER}}
DB_PASSWORD=${{MYSQL.MYSQL_PASSWORD}}
SESSION_DRIVER=database
CACHE_DRIVER=file
QUEUE_CONNECTION=database

   → Replace YOUR_KEY_FROM_LOCAL_ENV with your actual APP_KEY
   → Click "Save Variables"

6. Wait for Deployment (2-3 minutes)
   → Railway will build your app
   → Green checkmark = Success! ✅

7. Generate Public URL:
   → Click "Settings" tab
   → Scroll to "Networking"
   → Click "Generate Domain"
   → Copy your URL (e.g., minimart-production.up.railway.app)

8. Seed Database:
   → In Railway dashboard, click your service
   → Go to "Deployments" tab
   → Click latest deployment
   → Click "View Logs"
   → If migrations ran, great! If not:
     - Click service → "Settings" → scroll to bottom
     - Under "Deploy," find "Run Command"
     - Run: php artisan db:seed

9. VISIT YOUR LIVE SITE! 🎉
   → Open the generated URL
   → Your Mini Mart is LIVE!
